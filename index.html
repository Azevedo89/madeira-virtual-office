FAQ

The practical questions.

Clear information before you decide.

Is this a real physical address?

Yes. MV OFFICE operates from a physical business location in Funchal. It is not a PO Box.

Can I use it as my registered office?

Registered-office use is available where permitted and subject to the applicable requirements. We confirm the relevant requirements for your situation before activation.

What happens when my mail arrives?

Mail is checked daily. You are notified when correspondence arrives and important documents can be scanned securely and sent by email.

Is forwarding included?

No. Physical forwarding is an additional service, so you only pay for it when you need it.

What does the €300 annual membership include?

The core membership includes the professional address, mail handling and notifications, scanning, one coworking day per month with printer/scanner access, basic support and appropriate local introductions.

Do I need to live in Madeira?

No. The service is designed to support businesses and professionals who need a local Madeira presence, including international clients.

Can MV OFFICE provide an accountant or lawyer?

MV OFFICE does not replace specialist professional advisers. Where appropriate, we can help introduce you to independent local professionals who may be relevant to your requirements.

Can I request other services?

Yes. Additional MV OFFICE services can be arranged separately. The current Services & Price Guide sets out the available options and applicable charges.
